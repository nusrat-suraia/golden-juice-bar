Our Story

Bringing authentic, handcrafted beverages to the heart of London

"We believe that great drinks start with quality ingredients and a passion for flavor."

✨ Our Mission

Golden Juice Bar was born from a simple idea: to create a space where Londoners can enjoy authentic, freshly made beverages that are as good for you as they are delicious. We combine traditional recipes with modern twists, using only natural ingredients.

🌍 Our Journey

Founded in 2024, Golden Juice Bar started as a small stall in a London market. Our commitment to quality and flavor quickly gained a loyal following. Today, we're proud to serve our community from our flagship location at 802 Iona Tower.

💛 What Makes Us Golden

  • 100% natural ingredients — no artificial syrups or preservatives
  • Traditional preparation methods, from hand-pressed juices to sand-brewed coffee
  • A warm, welcoming space where everyone feels at home
  • Sustainable practices — we use eco-friendly cups and source locally when possible
